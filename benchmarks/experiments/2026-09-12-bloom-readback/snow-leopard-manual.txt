Temporary bloom-on launch
-------------------------

The same installed application was launched through Snow Leopard's supported
`open --args` path with `+set gl_bloom 1`, followed by live cvar queries. The
process stayed alive, but its 41-line qconsole stopped after OpenGL extension
probing. It did not print the normal Yamagi initialization completion, a map,
the requested cvar readback, or Bloom GL diagnostics.

A bounded process check after 1 minute 46 seconds showed the engine in state U
using 7.9% CPU. TERM was sent first and did not end the process after more than
10 seconds; KILL was then used as the documented backstop. The held canonical
reservation was released, zero Quake II processes remained, and the persisted
user setting was verified as:

  set gl_bloom "0"

The user did not report what the stalled bloom-on frame looked like. Do not
count this as a bloom visual test. The matched normal launch and bloom-on stall
place the next discriminator after extension probing and before map startup;
instrument the post-probe setup calls, especially `R_InitBloomTextures`, before
changing bloom behaviour.
